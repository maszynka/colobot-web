Privacy

Colobot Web is a free, open-source browser port of Colobot (source). You can play without an account: in local mode nothing about you leaves your browser, and your saves stay in this browser's storage.

What is stored if you sign in

  • Email and password account: your email address and a salted password hash (PBKDF2-SHA256). The password itself is never stored.
  • Google sign-in: your Google account ID (sub) and email address. We request only the openid email scopes.
  • Sessions: a random sign-in token in an HttpOnly cookie; the server keeps only its hash, for up to 30 days or until you sign out.
  • Saved games: a zip archive of your game's save folder (player profiles, saved missions, your CBOT programs, settings), with the last 10 versions, the upload time and a short device label such as "Windows Chrome".
  • Abuse protection: counters of failed sign-in attempts per IP address and per IP address + email, and of save uploads per account, kept for about 15–30 minutes.

Data is stored on Cloudflare (Workers, D1, R2). It is used only to run the game's save sync, never sold or shared, and there is no tracking or advertising.

Deleting your data

Sign in, open the account badge in the top-right corner of the game and choose Delete account and cloud saves, or use the button below. Password accounts confirm with the password; Google accounts must have signed in within the last 10 minutes. This removes your account, sessions and every cloud save immediately. Saves in your browser are kept; you can remove them by clearing site data.

Po polsku

Możesz grać bez konta — wtedy zapisy zostają tylko w tej przeglądarce. Po zalogowaniu przechowujemy: e-mail i skrót hasła (PBKDF2) albo identyfikator i e-mail konta Google, token sesji (jako skrót, do 30 dni) oraz archiwum zapisów gry (10 ostatnich wersji). Dane leżą w Cloudflare i służą wyłącznie do synchronizacji zapisów. Konto i wszystkie zapisy w chmurze usuniesz przyciskiem powyżej albo z menu konta w grze.
